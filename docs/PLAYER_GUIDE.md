# Player Guide — Premium V5

Begin as a noble or royal household member. Choose an era and appearance, then select a family background. A custom noble house can choose a particular strength and a burden such as debts, rivals or inherited disgrace.

## Daily life

Use the **Overview** to visit illustrated destinations, take activities, speak to NPCs and advance day/month/year. A day advances after an action, and the simplified story calendar continues to age the household. An important event can interrupt time advancement until a choice is made.

## Careers & ambitions

Select **Careers & ambitions** to choose among civil office, military command, scholarship and household leadership. Training gives experience and improves a relevant attribute, while scholarly practice also improves scholarship. Qualifications require experience, ability and money. A promotion requires qualifications, experience and—where appropriate—influence and royal favor. Changing careers costs money and resets that career's rank. Formal civil and military appointments are restricted in historical mode, with broader access in Alternative Joseon.

## Estates & investments

The **Estates & investments** screen records land level, commercial ventures, monthly obligations and household wealth. You can oversee accounts, improve an estate, fund a partnership, or sell an investment. Settlement occurs when the game moves into a new month. Income can be disrupted, while cost of living continues. Even a commoner household may earn modest livelihood through work.

## Culture & Society

Seasonal invitations, poetry, ancestral rites, debates and patronage offer non-political sources of prestige, learning, friendship and conflict. Some invitations have social costs and require choosing between competing obligations. Major cultural accomplishments enter the family record and can survive generational succession.

## Loss of noble status

A formal royal trial may revoke legal standing. The screen **YOU ARE NOW A COMMONER** offers continuation or an ending. A continuing character may undertake restricted work, support relationships, investigate the accusation and petition for restoration. To appeal, build evidence and influence, wait at least 20 days and keep funds available. **An appeal can be refused**, and a restored household does not automatically receive its former appointment or estates.

## Family and heirs

Develop trust and affection, pursue an eligible marriage, plan a child, and wait approximately nine abstract months for the birth. Only an adult child can continue as a new playable generation. The household's traditions, records and legal standing shape the heir's starting circumstances.

## Save your game

Autosave stores progress locally. There are three named manual slots plus downloadable **JSON backups**. Import backups before continuing on a different site domain. Browser storage can be lost if website data is cleared. Export often.

## Accessibility and realism

The interface uses readable responsive layouts, focus outlines, off-by-default ambient sound and reduced-motion support. Locations and calendar are stylized abstractions. The historical campaign's rulers are contextual, while most NPCs are fictional.

## Premium V6 additions

**House Council & Identity:** Open the House Council tab to view birth/legal standing separately from honor, legitimacy, prestige, authority and family cohesion. Study one of four disciplines for a day, convene the elders, resolve rivalries or seek leadership. Some actions require age, influence, reputation, family resources or earlier progress.

**Personal cash versus household money:** In Estates & Investments, the original personal cash stat is not the family treasury. You may save personal cash privately, contribute cash to family funds, purchase farmland, repair holdings, invest in textile partnerships, repay debt or close ventures. Family headship is required to take cash *from* the family treasury into personal funds. Monthly accounts record expenses and income; debts persist after succession.

**Educating descendants:** In Family & Dynasty, descendants aged 5–17 can be taught classics, administration, martial strategy or the arts. The chosen preparation affects their abilities if you continue as that child after they become an adult.

**Formal office:** The Career tab now includes an official-service duty action for appointed characters with appropriate education. Strong political influence does not automatically entitle a character to exercise the monarch's power.
