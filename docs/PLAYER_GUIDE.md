# How to Play

The goal is to live a memorable life in Joseon, not to reach a single predetermined ending. Decisions affect reputation, royal favour, household wealth, alliances, romances, and descendants.

**Starting:** Choose Historical or Alternative Joseon, select a curated era or a custom historical year, create your character and house, and make your opening decision. An established noble house begins with reputation and scholarship; a political house with influence; a military house with courage; a royal branch with royal favour.

**Everyday life:** From Overview, choose one-day activities to study, manage your estate, attend court, gather information or rest. Move through six locations or use the stylized capital map in Court & Activities. Time can also advance by day, month, or year. Major narrative moments interrupt the flow and ask you to make a consequential choice.

**Relationships:** Open any character portrait to converse, give gifts, discuss politics or express interest. Different actions affect affection, respect, trust and romance. Only one interaction per NPC is available on the same day. Marriage requires a mature bond and adequate reputation. The Alternative campaign allows greater romantic freedom.

**Politics:** Visit Factions & Influence to pledge allegiance, gather information or broker compromise. Each action spends a day and temporarily locks the next council intervention for seven in-game days. Favour gained from one faction may cost standing with the others.

**Family & dynasty:** Marriage allows planning for a child. Pregnancy/childbirth is represented abstractly over approximately nine 30-day months. A descendant must reach adulthood before continuing the dynasty when a life concludes. Health and age matter.

**Legacy:** Twelve milestones track friendship, love, political influence, office, a new generation and other achievements. The Chronicle records what the court and your house will remember.

**Saving:** Progress autosaves after state changes. Save/Load provides three manual checkpoints plus portable JSON backups. Do not rely solely on browser storage before clearing your data or changing domains.

**Language & ambience:** Settings switches between English and Korean. The speaker button controls optional synthesized ambient sound; it starts muted.
